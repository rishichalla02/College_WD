Library

About Library

Central library of Shantilal Shah Engineering College, Bhavnagar offers adequate facilities and good quality resources to both, students as well as faculties of the institute. Central library of the institute is housed in a dedicated building with a floor area of 666 sq. m. on the ground floor and 506 sq.m. on the first floor, totalling 1172 sq.m. The library has a rich collection of text as well as reference books, digital resources such as E-journals and magazines, video lectures and web courses.

Library Hours

Library is open from 10:30 AM to 06:10 PM on all government working days, however, digital resources are accessible round the clock through campus wide area network. For issue of books, students are advised to follow the scheduled below.

  1. Submission of Requisition Slips : 11:00 AM to 02:00 PM
  2. Issue of Books Requisitioned on the same day : 02:00 PM to 04:00 PM

Reading Room Facility

The library has a reading room capacity of 50 students with very good ventilation and lighting. The building is equipped with Wifi facility to access E-resources and internet.

How to recommend a book for purchase?

A faculty/student can recommend a book for purchase if he/she is finds that it is not available in the library or feels that more copies of the book should be available. For doing so, he/she must click on the following link and provide complete details of the book recommended.

https://forms.gle/Lsibrpb6YwukFoS86

List of books so recommended, will be forwarded to the concerned Head of Department for consideration, as and when the purchase procedure is taken up. The purchase will be strictly as per the guidelines from institute authorities and mere recommendation from a student/faculty will not be a sufficient reason for purchase of a book.

Code and Conduct of Library usage

Daily newspapers and certain magazines are available for students and faculties.

Library is committed to offer resources and services to all the students and faculties of the institute in a best possible manner. To enable us in doing so, it is desired that a brief code of conduct be followed by each student and faculty visiting the library.

  • Keep silence while you are in and around the library building.
  • Help maintain cleanliness in the library.
  • Use reading room or Wifi facilities for academic purposes only.
  • Do not consume food items inside the library building.
  • While accessing video lectures, make use of headphones/earphones.
  • Please cooperate with the library staff in smooth conducting of library operations. Follow any instructions given by the librarian or institute authorities.
  • In case of any complaints/queries/suggestions, please draw attention of the librarian or faculty coordinators.
