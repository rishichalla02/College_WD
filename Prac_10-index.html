About Us

Shantilal Shah Engineering College is affiliated to the Gujarat Technological University. It is approved by All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi and Government of Gujarat. The institute started functioning from the academic year 1983-84. The government vows to run the institute in nice manner and it intends to impart the best education by personal attention to each student. This has helped students to develop their personality and thereby making them more compatible with the industry standards. Students of SSEC comprise cream of the young generation who are intellectually ready to accept the challenges of life in general and the industry in particular, in the coming decades.
